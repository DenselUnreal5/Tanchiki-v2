Row 3: (1) concrete jersey barrier, long horizontal overhead shape; (2) square low military base bunker with reinforced steel roof, hatch, no turret; (3) burnt-out tank wreck with broken tracks, detached buckled turret resting on hull, no fire; (4) short streetlamp overhead with narrow steel pole fixture, amber lamp cap and tiny base.
